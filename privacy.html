Privacy Policy

Color Sort (app ID com.maxdflame.colorsort) · Last updated 21 October 2026

Color Sort is a puzzle game. You don't need an account, and we don't ask for your email, phone number, contacts or location. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and offers an optional "Remove ads" purchase (both explained below). This page explains the information the game handles.

Stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, shop items, achievements, daily-challenge streak and settings are saved on your phone. Settings never leave the phone; your progress is also backed up to our server (see the next section). Uninstalling the app, clearing its data, or using Settings → Reset progress deletes the copy on the phone.

Sent to our server

A few features need a server: the daily leaderboard, your player profile and your stats history. For these the game creates a random player ID when it's first opened. It isn't linked to your name, your Google account or your phone's hardware IDs. The game sends:

Daily reminders are scheduled on the phone itself and send nothing to us.

Your backup is stored under your save code (shown in Settings → Cloud Save). We keep only a scrambled (hashed) form of the code, so the backup can only be found by someone who has the code itself. Keep it private.

This data is stored by our database provider, Supabase, and is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Your nickname and daily scores are visible to other players on the leaderboard; everything else is not. We don't sell this data or share it with anyone else, and we don't use it for advertising.

Ads

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: short videos you can choose to watch for a reward (a free hint, extra moves, double coins), and now and then a full-screen ad between levels. To show and measure ads, Google's ad software collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which gives a rough location), information about ad interactions, and crash and performance details. We never receive any of this.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads). Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and up.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is bought through Google Play, which handles payment; we never see your card details. Our server checks the purchase with Google and keeps a scrambled (hashed) form of its purchase reference with your random player ID. Google Play remembers the purchase, so Settings → Restore purchases brings it back on a new phone.

How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries, profiles, stats and backups are kept while the game is running, so you can see your history. Game statistics and error reports are deleted after 12 months.

Deleting your data

To have your server data deleted, email us at maxdflamegaming@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Color Sort data". Include your nickname, your save code if you want your backup deleted, and, if you can, the date of a daily score you submitted. We'll delete your profile, scores, stats, statistics and backup within 30 days. Data stored only on your device can be removed by uninstalling the app or using Reset progress.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted a nickname, contact us and we'll remove it.
